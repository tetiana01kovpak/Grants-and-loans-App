# Funding research — AI marine navigation

Research date: 12 November 2026. Funding statuses change. This is a starting shortlist, not a complete inventory or a confirmation of eligibility. Review the official documents before applying.

## Your project

Your company develops devices for sailing boats and yachts that use AI and data to support autonomous navigation and docking. Potential themes include blue economy, smart maritime mobility, autonomous systems and deep tech. The company's registration, location, team size, finances, technical maturity, test results and partnerships were not provided; these affect eligibility.

## Opportunities

### SEAS: sustainable enterprises advancing blue solutions

- Potential fit: Croatian and Italian SMEs working together on innovative blue-economy products. Autonomous navigation or docking could be relevant if it meets the detailed criteria.
- Funding: €80,000–€125,000 grant per project, up to 80% of eligible costs.
- Partnership: at least one Croatian and one Italian entrepreneur; maximum three partners, in eligible cross-border areas.
- Timing: first cut-off was 9 November 2026. Additional cut-offs depend on demand and funding; do not assume another will open.
- Preparation: joint proposal in English, partner and company information, project budget, co-financing evidence and official forms. Confirm the mandatory documents in the guidelines.
- Source: https://cbchb.eu/seas-call-for-grant-applications-for-entrepreneurs-from-the-croatian-italian-cross-border-area-is-now-open/

### Baltic Marine Watchdog Challenge

- Potential fit: autonomous navigation technology as part of a team that can deliver maritime surveillance; yacht parking alone does not satisfy the challenge.
- Funding: up to €520,000 per team across two stages, through pre-commercial procurement.
- Deadline: 2 December 2026, 14:00 Swedish local time. Confirm the time and current status with the source.
- Application: a legal entity submits an online form and a mandatory Declaration of Honour; claims must be supported with data or identified estimates. Read the full conditions, including demonstrations and technical requirements.
- Source: https://www.vinnova.se/en/calls-for-proposals/radikal-innovation/the-baltic-marine-watchdog-challenge/

### EIC STEP Scale Up

- Potential fit: eligible companies scaling strategic technologies, including AI and autonomous systems. Investment scale and other requirements make this suitable only for a sufficiently mature company.
- Funding: €10–30 million equity investment, not a grant. The 2026 batch date is 25 November 2026.
- The 2026 work programme requires a business plan (up to 50 pages), pitch deck (up to 15 pages), qualified investor pre-commitment in the official template, financial plan, freedom-to-operate analysis and key personnel CVs. The pre-commitment and target funding-round conditions are substantial; read the official call.
- Sources: https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/step-scale_en and https://eic.ec.europa.eu/document/download/52598755-1351-4b54-b46b-e2682d0a3aec_en

## Useful preparation documents

Depending on the call, gather company registration and ownership records, financial accounts, project and technical descriptions, development and testing evidence, a detailed budget, co-financing evidence, team CVs, intellectual-property information, business plan, and partner details or letters. Prepare evidence specifically for your marine device: prototype status, navigation and docking test results, safety approach, target customers and plans for sea trials. These are suggestions, not a universal mandatory checklist; use each call's current official forms and instructions.
